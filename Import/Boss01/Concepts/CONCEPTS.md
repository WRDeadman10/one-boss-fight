# Boss 01: The Ember Warden — Silhouette Concept Proposals

Authority: `Documentation/ASSET_REQUEST_BOSS01.md` Section 2 & `Documentation/AG/BRIEF_01.md` Task T2.
Model Target: 310 cm final in-game height (authored at native mannequin scale ~181 cm, mesh component scale 1.5x–1.7x in engine).
Palette: Blackened plate, dull iron, crimson cloth with antique gold trim, ember orange glow in armor seams, visor, and blade.
Dormant Pose: Standing hunched vigil, two-handed greatsword (`SM_Boss01_Greatsword`) planted point-down into the cracked arena flagstones, hands resting on the pommel cap.

---

## Concept 1: The Ash Crown Sentinel (Classic Dark Fantasy / Ancient Sovereign)
- **Files**: `Concept1_AshCrown_Front.png`, `Concept1_AshCrown_Side.png`, `Concept1_AshCrown_25mFog.png`
- **Silhouette & Design**: A tragic fallen monarch sentinel. The head is crowned with a jagged, six-pointed coronet fused directly into an iron bascinet, evoking the broken spires of a scorched cathedral. The shoulder line features strong asymmetry: an oversized, 3-tiered spade pauldron on the weapon-bearing right side with an upward-pointing blade fin, balanced by a sleeker, curved plate pauldron with an ember-etched groove on the left shoulder to allow clean silhouette clearance when telegraphing the off-hand A5 Ember Javelin throw. A ribbed gothic cuirass with an ember fissure down the sternum leads into segmented plate faulds and a heavy, floor-sweeping crimson cloak edged in weathered antique gold trim.
- **25m Fog Readability**: The crown spikes create an unmistakable jagged top profile against backlighting, while the oversized right pauldron immediately signals the sword arm's threat axis even in dense arena mist.

---

## Concept 2: The Horned Bulwark (Archaic Executioner / Heavy Slab Iron)
- **Files**: `Concept2_HornedBulwark_Front.png`, `Concept2_HornedBulwark_Side.png`, `Concept2_HornedBulwark_25mFog.png`
- **Silhouette & Design**: An archaic, immovable fortress executioner. The helmet is a brutal cylindrical greathelm flanked by monumental curling iron ram horns that sweep back and outward, with a wide horizontal furnace grill visor glowing with molten interior heat. Symmetrical monumental fortress pauldrons with high vertical neck-guard mantlets frame the head, giving the upper torso an imposing, broad wedge silhouette. The chest is armored in heavy riveted iron slabs with horizontal glowing heat seams, anchored by wide dual-slab split faulds and heavy cylindrical greaves. Rather than a single cape, a dual split war-mantle drapes over both shoulders, cascading down like two scorched crimson wings.
- **25m Fog Readability**: The wide sweeping horns and massive rectangular neck mantlets produce the most intimidating, widest silhouette of all three concepts, reading instantly through fog as an immovable heavy juggernaut.

---

## Concept 3: The Scorched Inquisitor (Flared Spire / Blighted Heretic Warden)
- **Files**: `Concept3_ScorchedInquisitor_Front.png`, `Concept3_ScorchedInquisitor_Side.png`, `Concept3_ScorchedInquisitor_25mFog.png`
- **Silhouette & Design**: An elegant yet terrifying high-gothic inquisitor knight. The helmet is an ultra-tall tapering conical steeple bascinet rising into a needle spire (reaching 2.05 m native height / ~330 cm in-game), paired with a narrow cage visor emitting blinding orange furnace light. The right shoulder boasts an extreme razor-blade wing pauldron rising well above the ears, while the left arm features an articulated plate vambrace wrapped with a glowing brazier gauntlet designed specifically to telegraph the charging of the A5 javelin projectile. The torso exhibits an exaggerated gothic wasp-waist cuirass with a razor median keel and flared pointed hip faulds, complemented by an asymmetric heraldic crimson half-cape pinned over the left clavicle with an antique gold brooch.
- **25m Fog Readability**: The extreme verticality of the steeple spire helm and the razor wing pauldron give this concept the most distinct vertical contrast, making it instantly recognizable even at maximum arena draw distance.
