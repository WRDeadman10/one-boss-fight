## What the pack gives (audited 2026-10-21, `g3_list_weapon_assets.py`, `g3_dump_attack_data.py`, `pie_g3_probe.py`, `pie_dump_abilities.py`)
- The player's weapon abilities are `GA_Sword_Melee`, `GA_Claymore_Melee`, `GA_DuaSword_Melee`, `GA_Twinblades_Melee`; each has `AttackMontages`, `StrongAttackMontages`, `ChargedAttackMontages`, `FallingAttackMontages` (arrays) and a `Cost Gameplay Effect Class` (`GE_MeleeAttackCost`: Attribute.Stamina -15, shared by every weapon).
- Attack data is four shared assets: `DefaultMeleeAttackData` (power 0, light), `StrongMeleeAttackData` (power 3), `ChargedMeleeAttackData` (power 3), `MeleeKnockdownAttackData` (power 3). Every one has `PoiseDamage` 20; nothing reads it (KI-032). Today the boss loses 20 poise per light hit and 50 per hit of power 3 or more (the edited gate in `GA_BaseHitReaction`, `Scripts/poise_by_power.py`).
- The player's `BP_CollisionComponent` raises the event dispatcher `OnHit(HitResult)` for every hit of every trace target (hands, weapon); at that moment the trace target's `HitData` is still the PREVIOUS hit's: the hit data is built right after the event. One frame later it is right.
- The combat style asset (`SwordCombatStyle`, `ClaymoreCombatStyle`, `DualSwordCombatStyle`, `TwinbladeCombatStyle`) names the weapon; the player starts with the sword.
- "Jumping heavy" of the Freeze is the strong attack started in the air (`Scripts/air_heavy_selector.py`); it often lands after the player does.

## Open design points (decide at the row)
- Which weapon is "Strength" and which "Dex" in the table: Strength = `ClaymoreCombatStyle`, Dex = `DualSwordCombatStyle` (twinblades as an alternative); the sword stays the starter weapon with the Strength numbers until the owner says otherwise.
- Values the Freeze does not give (ground strong, charged, jump light): proposal in the table doc at M8, flagged as tunable.
- Stamina scale: the pack costs 15 per swing; the Freeze says 35 and 10 "confirm resource scale during audit": read the player's maximum stamina at M10.
