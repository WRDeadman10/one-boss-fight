# C-1036 PPM diagnostic hotfix

Lead's `P_C1034_PARRY` capture produced real A4 parries and recoil on both routes, but the player camera reported zero weighted blendables before, at and after contact, and the four frames showed no distortion. The pre-press frame was black, a first-HighResShot artifact. This is a failed PPM visual proof, not a successful Tier 2 completion.

Source path: `URPGPlayerParry::NotifyParried` calls `URPGBossLegend::HandlePlayerParrySlowMotion`; only if the configured grade policy accepts the parry does the legend call `URPGLegendSubsystem::BeginParryFeedbackPulse`, which adds the material to the **parrying player's `UCameraComponent::PostProcessSettings`** at weight 1. The native parry policy enables Perfect but disables Normal by default. In the C-1036 data log, the press occurred at A4 position 0.640 and parry contact followed about 0.15 game seconds later; a non-Perfect grade is a plausible explanation, not yet proven by a grade log. The material load or camera target could also fail.

Development-only logs now identify the actual parry grade, a policy-rejected feedback request, feedback material/target, blendable add/skip, weight/count, and restoration. The probe takes one throwaway HighResShot before A4 and uses a unique run folder so old PNGs cannot satisfy its pending-file check. It presses at observed active-window position >=0.71, closer to the known frontal contact. The four proof frames and player-camera blendable report remain. C-1037 found that the probe's legend slow-motion count call is not exposed to Python; it was removed. BUILD-257 proved the Perfect-grade material is added at weight 1 to FollowCamera, while the material renders black; Antigravity owns the asset correction.
