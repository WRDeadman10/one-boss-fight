## Comparison probe and acceptance

Before selecting, Lead runs the same no-change baseline on the live arena: hold the player near the boss while it circles on both sides at 150–250 cm. Log each tick's camera world position, actual spring-arm length, sweep hit component, boss capsule response, and a camera-inside-boss-bounds boolean. Keep the combat state fixed across options and record a short video with player and boss silhouettes visible. Include standing, attack recovery, dodge, phase-two aura, and wall/floor passes. The dynamic baseline confirms when and how often intrusion occurs; it does not itself choose a fix.

Lead-only telemetry is prepared in `Scripts/Editor/probe_camera_inside_boss.py`. It starts PIE, enters combat after the actors are ready, and samples the live player/boss/camera state for a bounded 30 seconds while the Lead manually circles in both directions at 150–250 cm and captures the named standing/recovery/dodge/aura/wall/floor segments. The marker includes the actual camera position and pivot-to-camera distance, target arm length, estimated spring-arm sweep hit component (offsets are unavailable in the current Python API), boss capsule `ECC_Camera` response, and whether the camera point lies in a visible boss-mesh component's world AABB. AABB occupancy is an approximation, and unreadable component bounds remain `None`; it is not a rendered-pixel visibility verdict. The script does not alter camera or collision settings. Offline geometry/marker checks are in `Scripts/Editor/test_probe_camera_inside_boss.py`; Lead owns DATA-default PIE and video evidence. Per C-1104, do not schedule a true-legacy camera rerun to restore parity wording.

## Lead's BUILD-266 dynamic orbit result (C-1081)

Lead ran the awake-boss orbit probe for 30 seconds under two nominal labels, both DATA-default per C-1099, with player distance 115–280 cm and camera yaw orbiting at 60 deg/s. With all options off, the run recorded 0 camera-inside-boss-mesh AABB samples and a 320 cm minimum arm length. Option A (`rpg.Camera.BossBlocks=1`) produced 30/252 and 28/251 inside-AABB samples in the two DATA-default arms; arm length fell to 10–108 cm, with the camera snapping into the player and boss mesh. In this tested orbit scenario A worsened the observed intrusion; Claude recommends dropping A. This is evidence for the tested scenario, not a general claim about attack/lunge frames.

Option B (`rpg.Camera.HideBossInside=1`) recorded 0 inside-AABB samples and 320 cm minimum arm length in C-1081, but the option did not trigger in that orbit. C-1083's BUILD-267 PIE then confirmed it fires (14 and 25 enter/exit pairs in two DATA-default arms per C-1099 in 30 seconds) but too often: world mesh AABBs include the mantle and sword and can hide the boss while the camera remains far from the capsule. The C-1083 hotfix now tests the camera point against the boss capsule expanded by a 5 cm enter margin and 10 cm exit margin, and holds a local hide for at least 0.35 seconds to reduce blinking. BUILD-268 PIE accepted B in two nominal arms, both DATA-default per C-1099: 11/13 hides per 30 seconds at 20–58 cm from the boss center, lasting 0.35–0.43 s; no distant false triggers or blinking were observed. Option B is ready for owner review, with attack/lunge and broader scenario checks still pending.

C-1083 also found the prior C option rotated only the spring arm, which diverged by up to 165 degrees from ControlRotation. C-1086 corrected the subsequent diagnosis: ControlRotation remained fixed; the bearing shifted 45 degrees per 0.2 seconds because the driver sprint-orbited at 180 deg/s. Source search found no per-tick player camera writer; Lead's Editor readback identified `BP_CameraModeComponent_C`, `BP_TargetingComponent_C`, and `BP_AdvancedPlayerController_C`, none of which wrote control in the sampled run. Codex changed manual grace to read the pack `IA_Look` Axis2D action value above a 0.25 deadzone, retaining the 0.35 second grace and ControlRotation-driven boom. Yaw follow uses error-proportional `FixedTurn` at `clamp(abs(yaw error) * 6, 60, 360)` deg/s. BUILD-269 passed in two nominal arms, both DATA-default per C-1099: view-to-boss median 14° walking / 27° sprinting (p90 25–37°), versus off median 82–90° (p90 154–164°); 1 s IA_Look input moved control 35° with C on, same as off, and follow reacquired to 14–24° in about 2 s. C-1090 and C-1105 provide bounded B+C attack-mode evidence above; no option is selected. Per C-1105, the open KI-037/KI-066 gates are owner choice and package proof. Raw runs: `Docs/BossCombatReview/Execution/Claude/P_C1079_BOSSBLOCKS/engage2_*`, `engage3_*`, and `B_C1083_LOCKON/`; combined attack runs: `Docs/BossCombatReview/Execution/Claude/P_C1090_ATTACKCAM/`.
