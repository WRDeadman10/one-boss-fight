# Production change proposal: camera view at melee range

**C-1044 decision on this proposal:** Lead's clean legacy/data PIE trace at 105/200/300 cm found a full ~375 cm pivot-to-eye distance against the 370 cm arm, `sphere_trace=None`, and the boss capsule's `ECC_Camera` response `IGNORE` on both routes. The boss-capsule collision branch below is ruled out for those open-ground samples. The extreme close-ups in BUILD-255 occur when the bot drifts near arena walls/pillars and the drawn boss fills the frame. Lead ordered no camera Source change. A lock-on composition or pull-back may be an owner-directed future option; no film-grade result is claimed from the open-ground probe. Existing wall/floor collision protection remains in place; any future composition change requires those runtime checks.

Lead C-1030 and Antigravity A-628 saw the boss mantle cover the frame around 105 cm player-to-boss distance in `P_FIGHTVIDEO/fight_review.mp4`. This hides footwork in play and in the review capture.

`URPGCameraRig::Apply` enables the spring-arm collision sweep on `ECC_Camera` with a 34 cm probe. `URPGBossLook::Apply` creates the displayed body, mantle, crown, gauntlet and sword followers with `NoCollision`; these follower meshes do not themselves block the probe. The live pack boss capsule or another blocking primitive may retract the arm, or the camera may have a clear probe path but be looking through the drawn mantle. The present video does not distinguish those causes.

**Proposed scoped fix:** In Lead PIE, capture the spring-arm pivot, desired/actual camera position, arm length, sweep hit component, and boss capsule `ECC_Camera` response at the 105 cm encounter. If the boss capsule is the sweep hit, have the live boss capsule ignore `ECC_Camera` while preserving its Pawn, WorldStatic and combat responses, then verify arena walls and floor still retract the camera. If the sweep does not hit the boss, use a lock-on composition adjustment at close distance so the lens stays outside the mantle silhouette; do not turn off spring-arm collision globally. Test standing, attack, dodge, phase-two aura, wall and floor cases at 105 cm and farther. An opt-in debug overlay can display pivot, camera and sweep hit before selecting the Source change.

This is a PRODUCTION CHANGE proposal. No collision or camera Source setting has been changed yet; the Lead should choose the branch based on the PIE trace. Capture and owner review should be repeated after the fix.
