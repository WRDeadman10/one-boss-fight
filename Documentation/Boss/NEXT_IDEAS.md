# Ranked Boss-Fight Improvements

Rank is a proposed work order. Costs are relative effort bands, not estimates. Each proof request closes a documented gap or exercises a feature that is not built or not yet witnessed. No new numeric design targets are proposed here.

1. **Add parry feedback for the player.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGPlayerParry.cpp`; authored parry animation, VFX, and audio assets under `Content/OneBossFight/` or the selected pack content path. **Why:** BUILD-150 has no parry animation or sound (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-150); KI-018 records incomplete hit-spark VFX. **Proof:** a live parry shows the authored response and plays its sound once, while the resolver still records `Parried` without player health loss.

2. **Witness parry against the pack boss in the real arena.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGPlayerParry.cpp`; the real-arena PIE driver and evidence under `Docs/BossCombatReview/Execution/Claude/`. **Why:** BUILD-152 proves the parry tag mechanism, but not the pack boss's own melee reaction; BOSS-COM-004 and BOSS-HIT-008 remain PARTIAL in `Ledger/witness_status_v3.json`. **Proof:** the pack boss's weapon trace hits during the parry window, the pack reports the parry response, and player health stays unchanged.

3. **Choose and prove the real boss's parry posture/reaction policy.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGBossFrameworkProfile.h/.cpp`, `Source/OneBossFight/Boss/RPGFrameworkBossCharacter.cpp`, and the real boss profile asset. **Why:** the real profile still leaves `ParryPostureAmount` at zero; BUILD-150's PARRY value is a proof value equal to `PostureMax` (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-150). **Proof:** an owner-selected profile value is validated and a live parry shows its posture effect and subsequent boss state. Keep visual, animation, and sound decisions explicit.

4. **Give the gap-closer card a dedicated movement animation.** **Cost:** Large. **Files:** `Move_Sevarog_BandGap_MOV001` and the framework move-card/executor content path. **Why:** BUILD-148 uses an in-place HeavySlam-derived clip plus bounded warp; the available pack charge/lunge montages are not used (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-148). **Proof:** a live gap-band attack plays the authored charge or lunge, obeys its card timing and warp limits, and retains the measured range behavior.

5. **Prove the strafe budget across a real interruption.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Boss/RPGFrameworkBossCharacter.cpp`, `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGBossStrafeRules.h`, and a PIE proof driver. **Why:** BUILD-149b says the restart branch is logic-only; no live run staggers a strafing boss (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-149b). **Proof:** interrupt a live neutral strafe with stagger or phase hold, resume it, and show that the budget restarts and the boss later returns to card selection without a NoCandidate failure.

6. **Decide whether parry grades and stamina cost belong in the live player path.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGPlayerParry.cpp`, `RPGParryRules.h`, and the new `RPGParryGradeRules.h` / `RPGParryCostRules.h` pure rules. **Why:** BUILD-147 leaves stamina cost absent and treats the timing window as a single result (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-147). **Proof:** after an owner design decision, tests cover the chosen grade/cost contract and a live press applies it without changing the dodge-before-parry resolver order.

7. **Close the unbaked-window long-frame gap and detect stale bakes.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Docs/BossCombatReview/Execution/Claude/F7/bake_blade_path.py`, `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGBossMoveCard.cpp`, the move-card validator, and their tests. **Why:** KI-147 remains open for windows without baked samples; its existing coverage notes that the validator checks bake ordering/coverage but not whether the bake still matches the animation (`Documentation/KNOWN_ISSUES.md:258`). **Proof:** a controlled long-frame run passes for every damaging window, and changing the source animation without rebaking is rejected or clearly detected.

8. **Add boss death and dedicated phase-roar presentation.** **Cost:** Large. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Boss/RPGFrameworkBossCharacter.cpp`, `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGBossFrameworkProfile.h/.cpp`, and authored boss animation assets. **Why:** KI-152 records that death currently halts actions without a death sequence and the phase transition uses a stand-in clip (`Documentation/KNOWN_ISSUES.md:263`). **Proof:** live death plays once and stops combat; the phase transition plays the authored roar while the selector remains held for its duration.

9. **Make strafe failure detectable and recover once from being stuck.** **Cost:** Medium. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGBossStuckRules.h`, `Source/OneBossFight/Boss/RPGFrameworkBossCharacter.cpp`, and strafe automation/PIE coverage. **Why:** BUILD-149's only obstacle handling is the movement sweep, and the neutral-strafe detector is not yet wired to the boss (`Documentation/TEST_PLAN.md`, BUILD-149). **Proof:** a blocked movement scenario is recognized, direction changes at most once for the attempt, and unobstructed movement does not trigger the recovery.

10. **Capture the runtime overlay on a connected desktop.** **Cost:** Small. **Files:** `Source/OneBossFight/Player/RPGCameraProbe.cpp` and `Docs/BossCombatReview/Execution/Claude/B_QA001/OWNER_RUN_THIS.md`. **Why:** BOSS-QA-001 remains PARTIAL because the current disconnected session produced black captures; the owner-run recipe exists (`Ledger/witness_status_v3.json`, BOSS-QA-001). **Proof:** run the recipe in a connected game session and record a non-black game-view image showing the overlay's active move, phase, range, cone, hit window, and posture.
