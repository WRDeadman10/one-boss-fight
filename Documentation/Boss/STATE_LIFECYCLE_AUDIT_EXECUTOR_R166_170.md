## Unreset state and findings

1. **Likely per-generation counter carryover:** `HitsAccepted`, `HitsGuarded`, `HitsParried`, and `AppliedDamage` have no reset in `Rearm` (`cpp:96–105`). They are cumulative increments (`cpp:291–292,413–414`) and feed the boss's generation completion/result reporting (`RPGFrameworkBossCharacter.cpp:614–617`). After `RestartEncounter`, those values therefore include prior-generation contacts and damage. If the API/report contract is per-generation, reset them in `Rearm` and add a restart test; if they are actor-lifetime totals, the result field labels/docs should say so. No code changed by this audit.
2. **Intentional cumulative state:** `CleanupLogCount`/`ReleaseLogCount` are diagnostic counters; `HoldsInRun` is the deterministic hold sample index; `LastAbortedToken` is retained for the stale callback proof. These are not reset at the card boundary. Their actor-lifetime/generation semantics should remain explicit.
3. **Inert last-card values:** cached card/profile/anim, watchdog timeout, play-rate scalars, tracking telemetry, hold timestamps, cue metadata, warp snapshots, grab metadata and decision snapshot values are not all zeroed at every boundary. Their owning active flag/model state/montage/held pointer gates their use, and new-card startup overwrites the values before use.
4. **Teardown:** EndPlay delegates to `Abort("EndPlay")`, then `Super::EndPlay` (`cpp:603–604`). Object references are not manually nulled; UObject/component destruction owns their lifetime. Abort restores play rate, releases a grab, aborts the model, stops the montage, disables ticking and unlocks motion (`cpp:579–589`).
