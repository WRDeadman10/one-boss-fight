- IMPLEMENTED: relevant inspected implementation satisfies the stated behavior; gameplay feel still needs validation.
- PARTIAL: verified subset exists, but a specified part is absent, weaker, or not proved end-to-end.
- MISSING: positively established absence in the relevant implementation, not a failed filename search.
- UNVERIFIED: critical behavior resides in uninspected/current-unconfirmed assets or runtime evidence.
- NOT APPLICABLE: intentionally outside this single-player six-move design, with justification.

Evidence links resolve to exact files/line anchors. U01–U12 are exact-asset inspection packages in document 11, not claims of missing behavior. P0–P8 are roadmap packages in document 10. Effort: S <1 engineer-day, M 1–3 days, L 3–7 days, XL >7 days; estimates exclude art production and owner scheduling. For UNVERIFIED rows effort means inspection first; implementation estimate follows evidence. Priority ranks the gap/validation need, not how much of the requirement exists.

## Interpretation

These are not “percent complete” scores. PARTIAL covers both a verified limited implementation and remaining end-to-end proof; UNVERIFIED must never be translated into MISSING. In particular, normalized utility, adaptive memory, EQS-equivalent positioning and boss defenses cannot be rejected without U02/U03. Conversely, the late native ID allocation, current-radius-only ring, uncorrelated direct/splash test and narrow active-window polling are visible in the inspected source and do not require guessing Blueprint graphs.

The matrix does not demand all twelve Bellwarden example moves, a grab, EQS, Motion Warping, Epic GAS, StateTree or multiplayer. A4 natural selection is a project-specific missing integration within an otherwise partial attack-selection implementation, not a reason to replace the pack boss.
